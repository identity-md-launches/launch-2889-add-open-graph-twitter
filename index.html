Circle your mouse, trackpad or finger around your wheel. No need to click. Or press ← and → in turn, like pedals. Thirty seconds, most passages wins.

    His ghost replays passage times from the Squeekchain API's /v1/measurement/runs: in the API's words, "evidence signed by the device's key, not work certified by SqueekCore". His blocks are read from SqueekCore on Sepolia (blockInfo), which gives the cumulative work, here a passage number, at which each one completed.

    Care first. Squeektoshi runs when he chooses. Nothing here asks him to run, and nothing you do here reaches his wheel.
